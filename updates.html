1. Transparent WebM Video (.webm)

Uses WebCodecs VideoEncoder with alpha: 'keep' and webm-muxer to construct a genuine WebM video container with AlphaMode = 1 Matroska headers.

When uploaded to Canva, it goes straight to Uploads → Videos with full duration controls.

2. Transparent QuickTime Video (.mov)

Builds a 32-bit QuickTime Movie video container (.mov) with 24-bit RGB + 8-bit Alpha video tracks.

Native Apple QuickTime video format supported directly by Canva, Apple Final Cut Pro, Adobe Premiere Pro, and DaVinci Resolve.

How to use in Canva:

  1. In the Studio, choose Transparent WebM Video (.webm) or QuickTime Alpha Video (.mov).
  2. Click Export Transparent Video.
  3. In Canva, go to Uploads → Videos and drag your video file in.
  4. Place your subtitle video directly onto the Canva video timeline over your footage — pure transparent overlay with zero background remover needed!
